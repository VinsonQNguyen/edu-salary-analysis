## Power BI Dashboard — Education & Income Analysis

Developed a two-page Power BI dashboard to analyze how educational attainment relates to income levels across different age groups using the 1994 U.S. Census Adult Income dataset. This dashboard extends the original Python statistical analysis by translating key findings into accessible business intelligence visualizations.

**Tools & Technologies:** Power BI, Power Query (M), DAX, Python, Pandas, Data Visualization

### Dashboard Overview

**Page 1: Education & Income Overview**
- Analyzed the percentage of individuals earning over $50,000 annually across 16 education levels.
- Found that **29.59% of individuals with post-secondary education** earned over $50K, compared with **12.94% of individuals with a high school education or less**.
- Identified a **16.65-percentage-point difference** in high-income rates between the two education groups.
- Developed KPI cards and comparative bar charts to communicate educational income disparities.

**Page 2: Age-Group Analysis**
- Examined how education-related income differences vary across age groups.
- Identified the largest observed income-rate gap among individuals aged **50–59 (26.66 percentage points)**.
- Created comparative line charts and a detailed matrix displaying high-income rates and subgroup sample sizes.
- Highlighted differences in income patterns across demographic segments.

### Technical Implementation

- **Data Preparation:** Used Power Query to clean and transform data, standardize categorical variables, apply filtering conditions, and create custom education and age-group classifications.
- **DAX Calculations:** Developed reusable measures to calculate high-income rates, record counts, and percentage-point differences across demographic groups.
- **Data Visualization:** Built KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, and matrices to communicate statistical trends and support comparative analysis.
- **Analytical Interpretation:** Translated findings from the original Python analysis into business intelligence reporting, with attention to sample sizes and limitations.
